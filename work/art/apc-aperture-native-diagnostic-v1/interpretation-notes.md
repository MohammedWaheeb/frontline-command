# How to interpret the original-source probe

The probe's exterior ray aims at the **center** of each dark interior plate. A blocked center is evidence of solid hull behind that point, not proof that every visible pixel of the plate is occluded. In the original IR APC, the plate center is at absoluteY0.328 and its thickness is0.01, so its outward face reaches0.333, slightly beyond the un-beveled hull side at0.330. That outer face may still render as a dark mark after the door opens. The original native images must decide the readability issue; a center-ray result alone must not be described as a visible failure.

US and SA use a different arrangement: their dark plates remain inset behind the rear hull plane, and the source ramp rotation predicts inward motion. The separate arithmetic receipt is still only a prediction until the evaluated mesh/native probe runs. A sign flip alone would move each ramp outward but leave its end above the ground. Any correction needs measured panel/ground and hull clearance across its actual opening frames.

The diagnostic uses hull-only pose layers for APC openings. It cannot establish full body/turret composition or a complete gameplay boarding interaction. Original raw images, failed measurements and any later corrected candidate must remain separate.
