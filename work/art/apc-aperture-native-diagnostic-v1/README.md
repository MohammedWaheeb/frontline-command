# Pending APC aperture audit

Read-only source risk, prepared before the three untouched pending APC exports. The accepted SY shell correction and all other source remain unchanged.

US and SA attach their rear ramp above a floor hinge but rotate positive Y, which appears to move the panel forward into the solid hull. Their dark interior plates sit behind the original uncut hull prism. IR side doors rotate outward, but their dark plates also sit behind the solid hull. These are source findings, not a completed native failure diagnosis.

`render-diagnostic.py` is reserved for the sole Blender queue at a whole-asset boundary. It checks frozen inputs, measures actual evaluated outward movement and surface crossings, casts an exterior-to-interior-center ray against the hull, and renders36 original hull-layer poses: idle, first opening frame and last opening frame at four diagonal headings for each of US/IR/SA APC. The output explicitly excludes turret composition. No model changes, live writes, thresholds or frame counts are altered.

If native views confirm the problem, the smallest candidate should preserve exterior closed contours, running gear, turret, scale, anchor, hardpoints and all other roles while making a true interior opening and a correctly outward ramp. Such a candidate needs explicit evaluated parity and clearance/visible-aperture proofs before full exports; no blind bulk mutation is justified by this source audit.
