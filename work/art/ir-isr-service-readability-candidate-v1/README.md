# ISR service readability candidate

The exact Claude family review `29sept-aircraft-family-review-v1.md` found ISR parked and service too similar at native 1x. The parent authorized a bounded source candidate and two-heading comparison, with no full rerender until proof and native review. The original completed ISR source, 512-pose export and handoff remain unchanged.

This candidate adds the existing neutral Iranian maintenance case, feet, mast and articulated work light at `(0.45, -0.43, 0)` BU, forward of the wing on the port apron. It reuses the original IR fighter/gunship maintenance design; no new crew, ammunition, team color or fictional hose connection is added. The current ISR cover cycle, airframe, hardpoints, scale, anchor and state clocks remain unchanged. The rig is hidden from body and shadow outside `rearm`. The two source changes are one position-table entry and the condition that constructs the fixture.

`prove.py` is prepared, not yet run. It must establish exact evaluated old-object geometry and all original pose transforms/materials/visibility/hardpoints, 416 unchanged ISR non-service poses, 8,608 unchanged poses of the other ten aircraft, 96 additive service poses and 9,120 reverse resets. It also checks all service bounds and surface crossings against the original aircraft. Six native candidate service views at d03/d07 and f00/f03/f05 follow only after that proof. Compare against original parked and service at the same headings, plus the completed legacy IR strike service reference, at native 1x and 2x. A readable service cue is a visual gate, not inferred from object counts.

No Go or renderer selection changes: enemy servicing is never inferred from parked position or private owner information. The Claude review's speculative enemy inference is not adopted. This branch starts from ISR's own original frozen family source; the independent SA/strike crash fixes must be merged only through an explicit later equivalence proof if needed. No new full export is authorized by preparation alone.
